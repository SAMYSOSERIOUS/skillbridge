Which moves count as realistic

A move is shown only if O*NET's own Related Occupations lists it for your job, or it sits among your very closest skill matches — measured across the full O*NET skill profile, 19,000 task statements and at most one education-zone jump.

What "best trade-off" means

Every move is scored on pay gain, retraining effort and AI-risk change at once. A best trade-off is a move where you can't do better on one count without giving up another.

How your priorities are used

The set of best trade-offs is fixed. Your priorities only decide which of them we recommend first. Field sizes are real national employment counts (BLS OEWS), not how often people switch.

AI risk, honestly

Three independent indices (AIOE, OpenAI, Microsoft Research) shown as percentiles, with a note when they disagree. Disagreement is information, not noise.
