How this works

The frontier

Every move is scored on pay gain, retraining effort, and AI-risk change at once. A highlighted move is one where you can't do better on one count without giving up another.

Retraining effort

Skill-profile distance, how far you must level up, education-zone jumps (max one), and task similarity across 19,000 O*NET task statements.

Your priorities & proof

The frontier is fixed; your sliders only pick which frontier move is recommended. "Big field" and "Smaller field" reflect real national employment in each occupation (BLS OEWS) — how many people hold the job, not how often people switch into it. True mobility-flow data is a planned addition.

AI risk, honestly

Three independent indices (AIOE, OpenAI, Microsoft Research) shown as percentiles, with a flag when they disagree. Disagreement is information, not noise.

Data & limitations

O*NET skills, Job Zones & tasks (USDOL/ETA, CC BY 4.0) · BLS OEWS median wages · AIOE (Felten, Raj & Seamans) · "GPTs are GPTs" (OpenAI) · "Working with AI" (Microsoft Research). Profiles describe the typical job holder, not you; exposure is not certain job loss; licensed occupations need certifications the model can't see. Analysis of public data — not career advice.
